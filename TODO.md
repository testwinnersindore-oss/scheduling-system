
## Date scope and multi-dashboard outcomes
- The header-selected date is the single working context across Overview, Faculty attendance, Courses, Schedule, Scheduling Control, Master Data, Analytics, Notes and Audit; switching dates loads that date's saved records without overwriting another date.
- Date changes must not copy the previous date's faculty attendance, schedule slots, course data or edits into the newly selected date; future dates can be planned independently and historical dates remain protected from non-Master users.
- Scheduling Control uses the selected date's faculty/course mappings and shows relevant mapped courses without exposing a Primary/Priority label in schedule-facing records; faculty subject values are populated from Master Data.
- A user can be assigned multiple dashboards at creation or edit time, receives one access URL and credentials, can enter each assigned dashboard, and can switch only among their granted dashboards while retaining all role-specific permissions.
